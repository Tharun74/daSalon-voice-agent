# Serenity Luxury Spa - Sanctuary Etiquette & Wellness Policies

## Sanctuary Arrival & Quiet Environment
To preserve the peaceful ambiance of our sanctuary:
1. Please arrive 15 minutes prior to your treatment time. This allows you to relax in our herbal lounge, sip warm Ayurvedic infusions, and complete your personalized wellness consultation.
2. Mobile devices must be placed on silent mode upon entering the sanctuary.
3. Lockers, plush robes, and slippers are provided for your comfort.

## Pregnancy & Maternity Spa Policy
Guest and baby wellbeing is our utmost priority:
1. First Trimester: For safety reasons, no body therapies, massages, or heat treatments are offered during the first trimester (weeks 1 to 12).
2. Second & Third Trimesters: Customized Gentle Prenatal Massages and Radiance Facials are welcomed, provided the guest presents written clearance from their attending obstetrician.
3. Prohibited for Expecting Mothers: Deep tissue therapy, Ayurvedic Abhyanga, seaweed body wraps, steam rooms, and saunas are strictly prohibited throughout pregnancy.
