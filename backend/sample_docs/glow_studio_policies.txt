# Glow Studio Official Client Care & Policies

## Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
We understand schedules change! We require a minimum of 4 hours advance notice for any appointment cancellations or rescheduling. This allows us to offer the slot to waitlisted clients. Cancellations made with less than 4 hours notice or missed appointments (no-shows) may incur a 50% fee added to your next visit.

## Keratin Smoothing Treatment - Aftercare Instructions
To ensure long-lasting, silky, frizz-free results from your Keratin Smoothing Treatment:
1. Keep hair completely dry and loose for 72 hours (3 full days) following the treatment. Do not wash hair.
2. Avoid hair clips, bobby pins, headbands, tucking behind ears, or tying ponytails during the first 72 hours to prevent kinks.
3. Always use sulfate-free and sodium-chloride-free shampoo and conditioner.
4. Sleep on a silk or satin pillowcase if possible to reduce friction.

## Patch Test Requirement
For client safety and allergy prevention, a mandatory patch test is required at least 24 hours in advance for all first-time colour and chemical services (including Balayage and Highlights).
