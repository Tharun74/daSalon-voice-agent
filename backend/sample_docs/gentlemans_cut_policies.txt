# The Gentleman's Cut - Barbershop Rules & Grooming Tips

## Walk-In vs. Booking Policy
1. Appointments always take the first chair. If you booked ahead, your barber will be ready at your reserved time.
2. Walk-ins are always welcome. Typical wait times for walk-ins are 20 to 30 minutes on weekday evenings and weekends.
3. Monday: Closed all day. No walk-ins, no exceptions.

## Beard Care & Maintenance Tips
1. Use warm water to cleanse your beard and apply 3-4 drops of cedarwood beard oil daily to nourish the skin underneath.
2. Visit every 2 weeks for a professional beard sculpt to keep your neckline and cheek lines razor sharp.
3. Always finish a hot towel straight razor shave with our cold towel press and alcohol-free witch hazel tonic.
