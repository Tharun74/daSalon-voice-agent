# da Salon — business and product context

Researched 14 November 2026 from public first-party pages. This is a record of published offerings, not verification inside a subscribed account. Account, country, configuration and commercial terms may affect availability. No bookings, purchases, enquiries or account changes were made.

## Positioning and customers

da Salon connects business management, consumer discovery, recruitment and product procurement for beauty and wellness businesses. It serves independent operators and multi-outlet brands: salons, barbers, spas, nail/lash/brow studios, aesthetic clinics and related businesses. Partner centralizes outlet operations. The business site identifies Noida as headquarters and mentions Singapore operations, training and local Business Associate support. Its scale figures (8,000+ partners, 50,000+ customers, 15+ Indian cities) are company claims, not independently audited here. Partner is subscription-based; current prices require a quote.

Source: [Business overview](https://www.dasalon.com/business).

## Product structure

- **Partner:** operations, clients, staff, sales, catalog, offers, promotion and reporting for owners, managers and front-desk teams.
- **Connect:** recruitment and professional opportunities.
- **Market:** professional products and vendor relationships.
- **dasalon.com:** consumer discovery and venue booking.
- **Brand Homes:** customized customer-facing storefront examples built around da Salon's booking foundation.
- **Business Associates:** regional onboarding, adoption and growth support.

Partner separates Calendar from appointment lists; Sales from offer configuration; Catalog from Offers; and loyalty points from prepaid offers. Its six report families are General, Sales, Client, Staff, Appointment and Product.

Source: [Partner](https://www.dasalon.com/business/partner).

## Appointments, staff and resources

- Day/week calendars; venue, staff and status filters; online, phone and walk-in bookings together.
- Rescheduling, reassignment, conflict warnings, notes, cancellations with reasons, completion and no-show status; checkout from appointments.
- Booking lead time, future booking window, slot intervals, cancellation/rescheduling limits and closed periods.
- Simple, Roster, Full and Custom availability profiles; staff auto-assignment; resource-aware scheduling.
- Staff profiles, venue assignments, recurring shifts, time off, attendance, breaks and timesheets.
- Role-based module access, restrictions on client visibility, commissions and shared tips.
- Service prices, durations, taxes, staff-specific pricing, bundles and online/walk-in eligibility; rooms, chairs and equipment with capacity and hours.
- Service imports and account-enabled WhatsApp confirmations, reminders and service-due prompts.

Business benefit: coordinate capacity, staffing and bookings with fewer manual handoffs.

Source: [Appointments and staff](https://www.dasalon.com/business/features/appointments-and-staff).

## Sales, payments, inventory and reporting

- Combined checkout for services, products and prepaid offers; split/partial payments, unpaid balances, refunds, voids, taxes and PDF/A4/thermal invoices.
- Optional client/OTP checkout requirements and permission-controlled refunds.
- Online transaction records, venue balances, bank verification and payouts.
- SKUs/barcodes, purchasing/selling prices, venue stock alerts, suppliers, purchase orders, stocktakes and inter-venue transfers.
- Service-linked consumables deduct from stock when sold; Market purchases can enter inventory.
- Revenue comparisons, monthly venue targets, payment/tax/commission reporting, appointment heatmaps, cancellation/no-show value and prepaid liabilities; spreadsheet exports and emailed reports.

Commercial distinction: no booking commission does not mean all transactions are free. Published terms describe online prepayments released after service, minus MDR and a platform fee; venue walk-in payments have no da Salon platform fee/MDR. Exact rates remain unverified.

Business benefit: connect takings, stock and performance in one operational record.

Source: [Sales, inventory and reports](https://www.dasalon.com/business/features/sales-inventory-and-reports).

## Client relationships and loyalty

- Shared profiles with occasions, acquisition source, appointments, spending, outstanding amounts, notes and offer balances.
- Notes can capture preferences, formulas, allergies and patch tests; access is permission-controlled.
- Smart Tags segment regulars, lapsed visitors and offer holders. Excel import supports preview and duplicate-phone skipping.
- Service-linked intake forms, standalone forms, consent/signature fields, stored answers and service-due reminders.
- Loyalty points for purchases, reviews, bookings and visits; configurable expiry/redemption limits, up to five tiers, rewards and referral bonuses.
- Where enabled, Inbox combines WhatsApp Business, Instagram Direct and Facebook Messenger, with assignments, labels, notes, scheduling, broadcasts and response analytics. Channel reach restrictions apply.

Business benefit: maintain service continuity, identify clients to re-engage and reward repeat visits.

Source: [Clients and loyalty](https://www.dasalon.com/business/features/clients-and-loyalty).

## Memberships, packages, gift cards and promotions

- Memberships support prepaid credit wallets or discounts, eligible services, venue restrictions, validity, caps and online/counter sales.
- Packages track sessions, minutes or monetary value, with service groups and redemption balances.
- Gift cards support recipient details, messages, email notification, sale discounts and balances carried across visits.
- Promo codes support flat/percentage discounts, usage limits, minimum spend, dates, venues and birthday/anniversary notifications.
- Client records track usage; manual adjustments require notes. Expiry alerts and liability reporting expose outstanding obligations.

Business benefit: encourage future visits, receive prepaid revenue, support gifting and control promotional discounts. These are distinct mechanisms, not interchangeable names for loyalty.

Source: [Memberships, packages and gift cards](https://www.dasalon.com/business/features/memberships-packages-and-gift-cards).

## Marketing and promotion

- Ads Manager supports Instagram/Facebook campaigns, playbooks, local audiences, daily budgets, campaign metrics and a lead pipeline through conversion.
- Creative Studio provides editable promotional templates, brand assets, background removal, saved designs and social/print exports.
- Message Center supports WhatsApp templates, manual recipients, saved audiences, smart segmentation and account-enabled automations. Delivery consumes credits.
- Easy Share creates offer/service links and QR codes, including a shared multi-venue directory link; guides cover placing booking links on social profiles and Google Business Profile.
- Account-enabled Smart Pricing supports peak/off-peak rules by time, venue, service and staff, with fixed/percentage adjustments and performance tracking.

Business benefit: acquire customers, follow up on enquiries, reactivate clients and promote quieter appointment periods. Advertising spend, messaging credits and feature enablement need account-specific confirmation.

Source: [Marketing and promotions](https://www.dasalon.com/business/features/marketing-and-promotions).

## Consumer visibility and reputation

The main site exposes discovery by city and venue category, offers, featured venues and a venue map. Its city navigation includes Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata and Surat; this navigation is not proof of live availability at every venue.

Source: [Consumer site](https://www.dasalon.com/).

Partner supplies venue profiles with photos, services, team, hours, location and offers; online booking/payment depend on enablement. Existing listings can be claimed through the listed salon phone. Paid Featured/Recommended placements run for selected weeks. A monthly venue-level Trusted Badge tracks eligibility, without guaranteeing ranking or revenue. Reviews connect to appointments/sales and support replies, filters and reporting. Reserve with Google and Google Review Boost are described as account-dependent features.

Business benefit: turn discovery into bookings and give owners a structured way to manage reputation.

Source: [Online visibility and reviews](https://www.dasalon.com/business/features/online-visibility-and-reviews).

## Connect — hiring and skills

Businesses post vacancies and filter applicants by role, experience and certifications, for full-time, part-time or freelance work. Professionals discover roles and academy-listed training. Recruiters manage multiple listings and match applicants. Professionals receive free access with a weekly application allowance, with an option to unlock more applications; exact limits and employer/recruiter charges were not established.

Business benefit: a recruitment channel specialized for beauty and wellness roles.

Source: [Connect](https://www.dasalon.com/business/connect).

## Market — sourcing and suppliers

Partner users can browse, order, track and receive professional products, including bulk purchases, then add items to inventory. Categories cover skincare, haircare, makeup, tools and salon essentials. Market access is included with Partner. Vendors can list and manage products/orders; the ecosystem includes dealers, distributors, manufacturers, CNF agencies and companies. Displayed example prices are illustrative.

Business benefit: connect procurement and stock records, while giving suppliers access to salon buyers.

Source: [Market](https://www.dasalon.com/business/market).

## Franchise / Business Associate model

The franchise page promotes a regional Business Associate partnership for distributing and supporting the platform. Associates represent da Salon locally, onboard and train salons, encourage adoption, and support ongoing performance. Published benefits include exclusive territories, training, marketing/strategy support and dashboards for salons, team activity, commissions and KPIs. Advertised revenue sources include subscriptions/onboarding, featured listings, marketing services, hiring and product sales.

Interpretation: this is a territory-based platform partnership; the reviewed page does not establish a conventional franchise for opening a da Salon-branded physical salon. Investment, revenue shares, exclusivity conditions, contract terms and earning outcomes remain unverified. Promotional industry statistics and earnings language must not be treated as guaranteed outcomes.

Source: [Franchise / Business Associates](https://www.dasalon.com/franchise).

## Brand Homes / templates

The collection displays seven examples: Maison Élan, Atelier, Serein House, Paloma, Oru, Néroli House and STUDIO / 07. It advertises custom typography, color, motion and voice, mobile-first layouts, memberships/packages/gifts and services/prices/availability supplied by the Partner API.

The Maison Élan example demonstrates multi-location presentation, service menus, booking entry points, offers and FAQs. Its FAQ explicitly identifies the brand/house names and images as illustrative, and memberships/packages/gift cards as demonstrations. It says menu prices and availability are live, but the inspected menu returned a loading error. Therefore a successful live booking or purchase was not verified. Demonstration names, prices, addresses, testimonials and terms are not general da Salon policies or verified customer references.

Sources: [Brand Home collection](https://da-salon-templates.vercel.app/), [Maison Élan example](https://da-salon-templates.vercel.app/brand-home-1), inspected through browser UI after web extraction failed.

## Working interpretation and unresolved details

da Salon's central value is connecting the salon lifecycle: discovery, booking, service delivery, checkout, retention and performance review, supported by hiring, procurement and local adoption assistance. This is a synthesis of the published capabilities, not a measured ROI claim.

Do not assume every account includes Inbox, WhatsApp automation, Smart Pricing or Google integrations. Do not infer public API access, credentials, endpoint contracts, integration permissions or production reliability from the Brand Home API reference. Current subscription tiers, taxes/fees, messaging costs, regional coverage, support commitments, franchise commercials and Brand Home delivery/pricing require further evidence when relevant. No authenticated workflows were inspected.
